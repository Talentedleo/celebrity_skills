## 1. How I Made $2,000,000 in the Stock Market (1960)

**Link:** [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/How-Made-000-Stock-Market/dp/1614271690) | [PDF](http://www.r-5.org/files/books/trading/investment/Nicolas_Darvas-How_I_Made_$2_Million_in_the_Stock_Market-EN.pdf)

The defining book. Darvas documented how he turned $36,000 into over $2 million in 18 months during the 1957-59 bull market.

### The Box Theory

Darvas observed that stocks move in defined price ranges — "boxes." A stock trades within a box (e.g., $50-$55), then breaks out on volume. He buys the breakout and raises the stop-loss.

**Core mechanics:**
1. A stock is in a "box" when it trades within a narrow price range
2. Buy when the stock breaks above the box on strong volume
3. Set a stop-loss just below the breakout point
4. As the stock rises, new boxes form — raise the stop accordingly
5. If the stock falls back into the box, sell immediately

### Darvas's Reading List

Darvas read ~200 books on the market before trading. His key texts:
- *The Battle for Investment Survival* by Gerald M. Loeb
- *Tape Reading and Market Tactics* by Humphrey Bancroft Neill
- Seven other core books on speculation and markets

### Key Quotes

> "I do not guess. I act on evidence."

> "I concluded that Wall Street was nothing more than a huge gambling casino — with dealers, croupiers and touts."

> "The money I made was not luck. It was the result of a system."

## 2. Wall Street: The Other Las Vegas (1964)

**Link:** Out of print

A more philosophical book. Darvas compares Wall Street to a casino:
- **Chapter 2:** The Dealers
- **Chapter 3:** The Croupiers
- **Chapter 4:** The Touts
- **Chapter 7:** Playing in the Casino — My Selling Game

**Core theme:** Wall Street is designed to take your money. The only way to win is to have a system and follow it ruthlessly.

---

## 3. The Anatomy of Success (1965)

**Link:** Out of print

Darvas applies his trading discipline to life success. Key idea: the formula for success is the same in any field — study the rules, develop a system, follow it without emotion.

---

## 4. The Darvas System for Over-The-Counter Profits (1971)

Applied the box system to OTC stocks. Shows adaptability of his method to different market segments.

---

## 5. You Can Still Make It in the Market (1977)

His final book. Introduced "Dar-Card" method. Also discussed short selling — something he had never done but recommended for experienced investors in changing markets.

---

## 6. The Darvas Method — Core Rules

| # | Rule | Description |
|---|------|-------------|
| 1 | Box Breakout | Buy when price breaks above a defined box on volume |
| 2 | Volume Confirmation | Never buy a breakout without strong volume |
| 3 | Trailing Stop | Raise stop-loss with each new box |
| 4 | No Guessing | "I do not guess. I act on evidence." |
| 5 | Trends Only | Only buy stocks in strong uptrends |
| 6 | Avoid Weak Hands | Never buy stocks that are falling or stagnant |
| 7 | Paper Losses | Cut losses immediately when box breaks down |
| 8 | No Averaging Down | Never add to a losing position |
| 9 | Focus on Leaders | Buy the strongest stocks in the strongest industries |
| 10 | Telegram Discipline | Once an order is sent, never change it |
