# 02-conversations.md — Nicolas Darvas's Conversations & Media Coverage

> As a 1960s figure, Darvas left no podcast interviews. His "conversations" come from Time magazine coverage and modern analyses.

---

## 1. Time Magazine Feature (May 25, 1959)

**Format:** Major profile in Time magazine, pp. 84-85
**Link:** [Time Archive](https://content.time.com/time/magazine/0,9263,7601590525,00.html)

The article that made Darvas famous. Time profiled the dancer-turned-trader and his $2 million fortune.

**Key Coverage:**
- Darvas's background as a dancer
- How he used cables and telegrams to trade while touring
- The box theory explained to a general audience
- His extensive reading (200 books)

**Quotes from the article:**
> "I do not pretend to understand all the intricacies of the market. My method is based purely on the price action of stocks."

---

## 2. Time Magazine — Criminal Investigation Follow-up (Dec 1960)

**Format:** Article
**Link:** [Time Archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20071231121359/http://www.time.com/time/magazine/article/0,9171,873372,00.html)

NY Attorney General investigated Darvas, claiming his $2 million story was exaggerated.

**Allegation:** Profits were only $216,000, not $2 million.
**Darvas's Response:** Called the charges "cynically irresponsible" and "book burning by publicity."
**Outcome:** Court blocked the investigation as "unwarranted invasion of free press."

## 4. Key Themes

1. **Self-Taught Success:** Darvas had no formal finance training — proof that discipline beats credentials
2. **System Over Instinct:** His entire philosophy was based on having a system and following it
3. **Volume is Key:** Breakouts without volume were ignored
4. **No Guessing:** "I act on evidence, not hunches"
5. **Controversy:** The $2M figure was challenged but never disproven
