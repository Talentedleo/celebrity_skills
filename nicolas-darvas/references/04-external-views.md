# 04-external-views.md — How Others View Nicolas Darvas

---

## 1. Time Magazine (1959)
- Profiled the "dancer who made $2 million"
- Portrayed him as eccentric but legitimate
- Gave his box theory mainstream exposure

## 2. NY Attorney General Investigation (1960)
- Charged his $2M claim was "unqualifiedly false"
- Alleged actual profits were only $216,000
- Court sided with Darvas — blocked investigation as press interference

## 3. William J. O'Neil (Investor's Business Daily)
- Called Darvas a pioneer of momentum investing
- O'Neil's CAN SLIM system shares elements with Darvas's box theory
- References Darvas in *How to Make Money in Stocks*

## 4. Modern Trading Community
- Widely respected as a momentum trading pioneer
- Reddit debates his $2M claim but respects his method
- Steve Burns wrote *How I Made Money Using the Nicolas Darvas System* (2010)
- Considered a precursor to modern trend-following systems

## 5. Critics
- His system only works in strong bull markets
- The $2M profit figure remains controversial
- Some call it "buying high and selling higher" — which is exactly what it is
